# Changelog

## 1.0.5 — 2026-10-30

### Hide apps

- Swipe a row **left**, or open the app and tap **Hide app**.
- A hidden app leaves the list immediately; the snackbar offers **Undo**.
- Show hidden apps with the **Hidden** chip, or bring them all back from
  **Settings → Your marks → Unhide all**.
- Hiding only removes the app from the list — it is not uninstalled.

### Mark apps as patched

- Swipe a row **right**, or open the app and tap **Mark as patched**.
- The mark records the installed version. If the app later updates, the card
  shows **Patched · older build** instead of pretending the new build is patched.
- Filter to marked apps with the **Patched** chip.
- Remove marks individually with **Not patched**, or clear them all from
  **Settings → Your marks → Clear**.

### Also in 1.0.5

- Status, category, patched and hidden filters for installed and All apps.
- Morphe Manager styling and more motion.
- Version 1.0.5 (`versionCode 14`).
